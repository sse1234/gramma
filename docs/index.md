# gramma

*Scripture, beautifully set.* A free, open-source Bible study app for
macOS, iOS, iPadOS, Android, Windows, and Linux — with typesetting in
the tradition of Gutenberg and Knuth, synchronized side-by-side views,
Strong's tools, notes and marks, and sync between your own devices
without any account.
